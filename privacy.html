MHK Poster: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

MHK Poster is a private tool used only by the artist MHK (@mhk_1996) to publish their own videos to their own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Data we access

Only for the one TikTok account the owner connects:

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name, avatar (user.info.basic)
  • Profile details: profile link, bio, verified status (user.info.profile)
  • Account counts: followers, following, likes, video count (user.info.stats)
  • The account's own public videos and their view and like counts (video.list)
  • Permission to upload drafts and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish)

We do not access other users' data, messages or contacts.

How data is used

Only to upload and publish videos the account owner chose, and to show the owner how those videos perform. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored by our integration provider (Composio) to make API calls. Videos are stored in the owner's own Google Drive until posted. No viewer data is collected.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps).
