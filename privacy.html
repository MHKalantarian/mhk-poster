MHK Poster: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

MHK Poster is a private tool used only by the artist MHK (@mhk_1996) to publish their own videos to their own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Data we access

Basic TikTok profile info (display name, open ID) and permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account. We do not access other users' data.

How data is used

Only to upload and publish videos the account owner chose. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored by our integration provider (Composio) to make API calls. Videos stay on the owner's computer; no viewer data is collected.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps).
