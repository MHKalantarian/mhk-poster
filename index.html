Schedule and publish music videos to TikTok

MHK Poster is the publishing tool of the independent artist MHK (@mhk_1996). It plans each song's short videos on a calendar and posts them to the artist's own TikTok and Instagram accounts at the chosen time.

How it worksPrivacy

Features

Content calendar

Every video gets a date, time, caption and cover frame. A month view shows what is planned, posted or failed.

Direct posting to TikTok

At the scheduled time the video is uploaded with the TikTok Content Posting API and published to the connected account with its caption and privacy setting.

Upload as draft

Videos that need a final touch (sound, text, effects) can be sent to the TikTok inbox as drafts instead, to finish and post inside the TikTok app.

Performance report

After posting, the tool reads the account's public videos and counts (views, likes, followers) to show which video styles work best.

Captions and covers

Captions, hashtags and the cover frame are written once per video and reused on every platform.

Status checks

A daily check confirms the connection, the schedule and every video link, so nothing is missed.

How it works

  1. Connect TikTokThe account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves the requested permissions. The tool only ever acts on that one account.
  2. Add videos to the planEach finished video is added with its caption, cover and posting time.
  3. Automatic publishingAt the posting time the video is uploaded and published to TikTok (or saved as a draft), and the result is recorded on the calendar.
  4. Review resultsThe report lists each posted video with its views and likes, read from the account's own video list.
  5. Disconnect anytimeAccess can be revoked in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.

TikTok permissions we use

user.info.basicShow the connected account's name and avatar so the owner can confirm the right account is linked.
user.info.profileShow the profile link and verified status on the dashboard.
user.info.statsShow follower, like and video counts in the performance report.
video.listList the account's posted videos and their view and like counts for the report.
video.uploadSend a video to the TikTok inbox as a draft.
video.publishPublish a scheduled video directly to the connected profile.

FAQ

Who can use MHK Poster?

It is built for the artist MHK and used only with the artist's own accounts. It does not access anyone else's data.

What content is posted?

Only original music videos the account owner made and chose to publish.

Is my data sold or shared?

No. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I contact you?

Message @mhk_1996 on Instagram.
